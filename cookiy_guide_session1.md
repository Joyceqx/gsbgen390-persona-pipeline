## Research overview

- **Project name:** AVP-style life history interview for LLM persona (Park et al. replication)
- **Objective:** Collect a verbatim, story-rich life history and worldview interview transcript to support building a generative LLM persona of the participant, to be evaluated against held-out batteries (BFI-10, GSS items, and decision/consumer preferences).
- **Interview duration:** 15 min  ← **WRONG, should be 35–45**
- **Methodology:** one_on_one
- **Sample size:** 1
- **Mode:** video
- **Target group:** A single participant (the researcher) who will complete two interview sessions to support LLM persona construction.
- **External knowledge:** Park et al. (2024) adapted the American Voices Project (AVP) interview style for building generative agent personas. This study collects a first-session transcript focused on life history, values, relationships, worldview, and meaning. Downstream evaluation will compare the generated persona to held-out participant responses on BFI-10 (Big Five short inventory), select General Social Survey (GSS) items, and consumer/decision-making preference questions.

---

## Core research questions

1. What formative life experiences, environments, and turning points most shaped the participant's identity, values, and current life path?
2. How does the participant describe what matters most to them, where those values came from, and what they have changed their mind about over time?
3. How do the participant's close relationships and communities function, and how do these connections influence their day-to-day choices and self-concept?
4. What worldview does the participant hold about how society works, responsibility, and what is going right or wrong today, and what experiences underpin those beliefs?
5. How does the participant think about religion/spirituality, meaning, and mortality, and how do these beliefs or practices show up in their life?
