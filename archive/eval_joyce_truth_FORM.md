# Held-out Eval — Joyce's Ground Truth Answers

**Read this first.** This is the ground-truth half of a held-out evaluation, modeled on Park et al. (2024). The whole point is that **you answer these *before* I show you the persona's answers, the interview transcript, or any analysis output.** If you peek, the eval is contaminated and we lose the comparison.

**How to fill this out (target ~15 min in one sitting):**
1. For each item, replace the `[ANSWER]` placeholder with your honest answer in the format shown.
2. Don't second-guess or look up "average" answers. Pick what's true for you today.
3. If you genuinely don't know or refuse, write `SKIP` — but try to push through; the persona has to commit, so the comparison is cleanest when you commit too.
4. Save when done. **Don't share with me until I tell you the persona has finished its run.**
