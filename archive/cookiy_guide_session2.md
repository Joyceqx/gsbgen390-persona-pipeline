## Research overview

- **Project name:** Academic replication of Park et al. (2024) – Session 2
- **Objective:** Understand participants' work and career context, decision-making style, consumer and brand behavior, time use, risk attitudes, and near-term life-changing choices using recent concrete examples.
- **Interview duration:** 15 min  ← **WRONG, should be 35–45**
- **Methodology:** one_on_one
- **Sample size:** 12  ← **WRONG, should be 1**
- **Mode:** video
- **Target group:** Adults who can describe recent work, purchase, and decision experiences, within the past 12 months.  ← **drift from brief**
- **External knowledge:** Session 2 in a two-session academic replication of Park et al. (2024). Session 1 covered life history and values; Session 2 focuses on work/career, decision-making style, consumer behavior, time use, risk/tradeoffs, and forward-looking choices. Emphasis is on specific, recent stories, not abstract personality labels.

---

## Core research questions

1. How do participants describe their current work and career trajectory, and what tradeoffs shape their choices?
2. How do participants make small vs. big decisions, and what roles do intuition, data, and social input play?
3. What drives meaningful discretionary purchases, willingness to pay, and brand loyalty/avoidance?
4. How do participants discover products and form trust in recommendations, advertising, and other information sources?
5. What risks and tradeoffs are participants currently navigating, and what upcoming decisions may change their lives in 1–2 years?

---

## Screening

1. **What is your age?** (18–24, 25–34, 35–44, 45+ qualify)
2. **Which best describes your current work situation?** (FT, PT, self-employed, student, not working — all qualify)
3. **Have you made a discretionary purchase over $200 in the past 12 months?** (Yes within 3/6/12 mo qualifies)
4. **Are you comfortable discussing how you decided and the approximate price for that $200+ purchase?** (Yes qualifies)

## Interview flow

### 1. Warm-up and bridge from Session 1 to recent concrete decisions
*"Thanks for joining again. Today we'll focus on how you make decisions in work and life, including recent purchases and tradeoffs. I'll ask about specific recent examples; share only what you're comfortable sharing."*

- **Q1:** Since our last session, what's one decision you made that took noticeable mental energy?
  - When did this happen, and what was at stake for you?
  - What options did you consider?
  - What information did you look at, if any?
  - What made you finally commit to one option?
- **Q2:** Earlier you shared values and background. What part of that shows up most in your choices lately?
  - What's the most recent situation where you noticed it shaping your behavior?
  - What did you do differently because of it?
  - Was there any tension between that value and practical constraints?

### 2. Work and career: role, fit, trajectory, side projects
*"Next I'd like to understand your work and career context through specific recent moments, not just your job title."*

- **Q3:** What do you do day to day in your current work or main occupation?
  - What did you do yesterday or the last day you worked?
  - Who do you work with most closely, and how?
  - What outputs or results are you responsible for?
- **Q4:** What parts of your work have you enjoyed most in the past month?
  - Tell me about one specific moment that stands out.
  - What exactly were you doing, and why did it feel good?
  - Compared to a typical day, what was different?
  - What does that suggest you want more of in your work?
- **Q5:** What parts of your work have been most frustrating recently?
  - Walk me through the last time this happened.
  - What was the immediate consequence for you or others?
  - Compared to a good day, what's missing in that situation?
  - How do you usually cope or work around it?
- **Q6:** How did you end up in this line of work? Start from the last major step.
  - What alternatives were you considering at the time?
  - What information or signals mattered most?
  - Who influenced you, if anyone?
- **Q7:** Are you driving any side projects, a business, or research outside your main role?
  - What is it, and what stage is it in right now?
  - What motivated you to start it?
  - What tradeoffs does it create with time or money?
- **Q8:** Looking ahead, what do you think your next career step will be, and why?
  - What would make you stay on your current path?
  - What would push you to change direction?
  - What risks feel most real in that next step?

### 3. Decision-making style: small vs. big, intuition vs. data, regret
*"Now I'll ask about how you make decisions. I'm less interested in labels and more in reconstructing what you did in real situations."*

- **Q9:** Think of a small decision from the last week. How did you choose what to do?
  - What were the options, and how did you narrow them down?
  - How long did it take from start to finish?
  - What would have made you rethink it?
- **Q10:** Tell me about a big decision you made in the past year and walk me through it end to end.
  - What started the decision—what changed or prompted it?
  - Who was involved, and what role did each person play?
  - What information did you trust most, and why?
  - What was the moment you felt it was decided?
  - Looking back, what do you feel best and worst about?
- **Q11:** When you're deciding, how do you balance intuition and data in practice?
  - Most recent decision relying mainly on intuition?
  - Most recent decision relying mainly on data?
  - How do you decide when you have enough information to stop?
  - Cases where intuition and data conflicted? What did you do?
- **Q12:** How often do you regret decisions, and what kinds tend to stick with you?
  - Tell me about your most recent decision you regret.
  - What did you miss or misread at the time?
  - Tell me about a recent decision you feel good about—why?
  - Did you change your process afterward? If so, how?

### 4. Money and purchases: discretionary spend, value, willingness to pay
*"Next are questions about money and purchases. You can keep details approximate—what matters is how you decided and what it meant to you."*

- **Q13:** How would you describe your relationship with money right now?
  - What's one behavior that reflects that relationship?
  - Has anything changed about it in the last year?
  - What expenses do you watch most closely, and why?
- **Q14:** Tell me about a meaningful discretionary purchase over $200 you made recently. ✅ **(eval-relevant)**
  - What exactly did you buy, and when?
  - What triggered the purchase in the first place?
  - What alternatives did you consider, if any?
  - Where did you look for info or reassurance?
  - What sealed the decision to buy that one?
- **Q15:** What will you pay extra for, and what do you refuse to pay extra for?
  - Last time you paid extra—what was it, and why?
  - Last time you chose the cheaper option—why?
  - How do you decide whether the premium is worth it?
  - Is there a price point where you usually pause or reconsider?

### 5. Brands, advertising, recommendations
*"Now I'll ask about brands, advertising, and how you hear about new products or services."*

- **Q16:** What brands do you feel loyal to, and what earned that loyalty?
  - Which specific brand is strongest for you, and why?
  - Most recent time you chose it over alternatives?
  - What would cause you to switch away from it?
- **Q17:** Are there brands you avoid? What happened that led to that?
  - Personal experience or hearsay?
  - Walk me through the last time you actively avoided it.
  - What would need to change for you to reconsider?
- **Q18:** How do you usually discover new products or services you end up trying?
  - Most recent new thing you tried—how did you first hear about it?
  - What made it feel credible or worth trying?
  - How long was it from first hearing about it to trying it?
- **Q19:** How skeptical are you of advertising, and what makes you trust or dismiss it?
  - Tell me about a specific ad you remember from recently.
  - What did you do after seeing it, if anything?
  - What signals make an ad feel more believable?
  - What are your red flags that make you dismiss it?
- **Q20:** How often do you recommend things to friends, and what makes you share or stay quiet?
  - Last thing you recommended—what was it and to whom?
  - What made you feel comfortable recommending it?
  - Time you didn't recommend something you liked—why not?

### 6. Time use and lifestyle
*"Next are questions about how you spend time and what your lifestyle looks like in practice."*

- **Q21:** What does an ideal weekend look like for you right now?
  - If I followed you for that weekend, what would I see you doing?
  - Who would you spend time with, if anyone?
  - What needs does that weekend meet for you?
- **Q22:** Walk me through your most recent weekend—what actually happened?
  - Planned vs. spontaneous?
  - What did you choose not to do, and why?
  - What part felt most restorative or draining?
- **Q23:** How do you usually spend free time on a normal weeknight?
  - Last night after work/school?
  - What pulls you into that routine?
  - What disrupts it when life gets busy?
- **Q24:** Have there been any recent lifestyle changes? What drove the change?
  - When did you first notice the shift?
  - What tradeoffs did the change create?
  - What has been better, and what has been worse?

### 7. Risk and tradeoffs
*"Now I'll ask about risk and tradeoffs. I'll focus on specific decisions rather than general attitudes."*

- **Q25:** Tell me about a recent tradeoff you made involving money, time, or energy.
  - Two sides of the tradeoff, in your words?
  - What did you give up, and how did that feel?
  - What information or advice did you use?
  - Looking back, would you make the same call?
- **Q26:** What's a recent situation where you took a risk—financial, career, or social?
  - Downside you were trying to avoid?
  - Upside that made it worth considering?
  - How did you reduce the risk, if at all?
  - Who knew about the decision, and did that matter?

### 8. Forward-looking
*"To wrap up, I'll ask about the next 1–2 years—decisions you expect to face that could meaningfully change your life."*

- **Q27:** What choices do you expect to make in the next 1–2 years that might change your life?
  - What would make that decision feel urgent or unavoidable?
  - What options do you see right now?
  - What would a good outcome look like to you?
  - What would you regret not considering?
- **Q28:** How are you preparing for those choices, and what information do you still wish you had?
  - Who are you talking to about it, if anyone?
  - What have you already done to get clarity?
  - What's the biggest unknown right now?
  - What new evidence would shift your plan?
- **Q29:** Is there anything important about how you make choices that we didn't cover today?
  - Recent example where that showed up?
